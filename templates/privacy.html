Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly to track your spending, we collect the expenses, categories, amounts, and dates you choose to enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the core functionality of Spendly — logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party services. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery) solely to operate the service, and these providers do not have independent rights to use your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
